Before you ask

Straight answers.

No runaround. If a detail matters, it is stated here or confirmed at checkout.

What exactly am I renting?

A private Windows or Linux VPS in a USA location — dedicated CPU, NVMe storage, unlimited bandwidth, and full admin rights on your own machine.

Do I really get full admin rights?

Yes. Windows machines give you administrator access and Linux machines give you root. You install and configure what the work needs.

Which operating systems are available?

Windows Server plus the main Linux families — Ubuntu, Debian, CentOS, Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux, Fedora, FreeBSD, and Alpine Linux. The machine is yours to configure after setup.

How does checkout and delivery work?

You continue to WHMCS for secure checkout. Pricing and availability are confirmed there. Credentials and account access come from the client area after the order.

Can I upgrade later?

Plans are listed on this page, and the client area is the place to manage services and billing. Pricing and availability are confirmed at checkout.
