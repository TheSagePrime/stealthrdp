Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. What services does StealthRDP offer?

Source: Verified StealthRDP documentation snapshotSource date: Jan 27, 2025Migrated 2026-08-13Public examples redacted
  1. What services does StealthRDP offer?

StealthRDP specializes in providing robust Remote Desktop Protocols (RDPs), Virtual Private Servers (VPS), and web hosting solutions tailored to meet a variety of cloud-based needs.

  1. How do I sign up for StealthRDP services?

You can sign up through our website, StealthRDP.com. Simply choose your desired service, complete the payment process, and follow the instructions to set up your account.

  1. What payment methods are accepted by StealthRDP?

We currently accept Cards and cryptocurrency as a form of payment. Keep an eye on our website for any updates regarding additional payment methods.

  1. Is there a trial period for your services?

StealthRDP does not offer a trial period. However, we ensure the high quality and reliability of our services from the onset.

  1. Can I upgrade or downgrade my service plan?

Yes, service plan modifications can be made. Please contact our support team for assistance with upgrading or downgrading your plan.

  1. What should I do if I experience technical issues?

Please contact our support team immediately, We are available around the clock until 5:30 PM MST. Rest assured that we strive to respond to your ticket within a quick timeframe of 10-30 minutes from the moment it is raised.

  1. How is my data protected with StealthRDP?

We prioritize data security. Our services include robust security measures to protect your data, though we also recommend that customers regularly back up their data.

  1. Can I cancel my service at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your service at any time. Please refer to our Termination of Service policy for details regarding cancellations.

  1. What happens if I violate the Terms of Service?

Violating the Terms of Service can result in suspension or termination of your services. We enforce our policies to ensure fair and legal use of our services.

  1. How can I get a refund?

Our services are generally non-refundable, except in specific cases of service downtime due to internal issues. Refund requests under these circumstances are subject to review and approval.

  1. How and when will I receive my service credentials?

Your service credentials are delivered via email within 5-10 minutes, depending on the OS installation process. Ensure that you check your email, including the spam folder, for these details.
