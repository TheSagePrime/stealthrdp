What happens after you choose a plan

  1. Compare the available resources and region.
  2. Select the Linux option in the checkout flow.
  3. Complete the order through the existing StealthRDP checkout.
  4. Follow the service instructions provided after purchase.
  5. Contact support when you need help with the service process.

Which Linux VPS plan should you choose?

  • Choose a lower resource plan for a small workload or light application.
  • Choose more RAM when your software or users need additional memory.
  • Choose more CPU cores for workloads that run several processes.
  • Choose more storage when your files, databases, or applications need more space.
  • Choose the region that best matches your users and operating requirements.

Support and customer responsibilities

Keep your account details secure, maintain the software you install, choose resources that fit your workload, and follow the service requirements for your intended use. Review the service terms before ordering and contact support for account or server-specific questions.
