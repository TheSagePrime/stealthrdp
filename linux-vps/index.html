Environment

If you need Ubuntu on a VPS

The live FAQ lists Ubuntu 18.04, 20.04, and 22.04, plus CentOS 7, 8, and Stream, Debian 10 and 11, and other distributions. Confirm the image in checkout. Do not assume Ubuntu 24.04 unless checkout shows it.
